## 2. The single most important finding for design

The client has **two eras of media** and they do not match:

1. **A new 2026 professional package** (studio headshots, a 5-patient testimonial shoot delivered in every aspect ratio, a polished ebook mockup, two brand wordmarks). This is premium and reusable.
2. **A 2019 WordPress site** built on a stock dental template (PBHS), padded with soft ~800px video frame-grabs, generic dental stock, and two AI-generated hero graphics.

**The brand names that drive the entire video library, "DreamSmile" and "The Burns Protocol", do not appear anywhere on the current website.** The new site should be built around them, leading with full-arch implants / Teeth-In-A-Day, using the 2026 photo and video package as the visual spine.

## 7. Content-strategy read

The library is a **full-arch dental-implant authority engine** on two proprietary names, **DreamSmile** (the outcome) and **The Burns Protocol** (the method). The base layer is education (~23 explainer shorts). Meet-the-doctor content (Dr. Burns, Dr. Josephine Burns) and the "Dynamic Duo" add the human layer.

**Recurring patients are the spine of the proof story.** The 2026 shoot cohort (Mike, Ron, Danny, Kelly, Angela) is the most polished. **Ready-to-place core for the new site:** the 2026 zip testimonials (all lengths), the MN before/after TikToks, the two landscape 4K brand/lecture films, and the ~23 educational shorts as an on-page education/FAQ library.
