What root canal therapy actually does

A root canal saves a tooth that would otherwise be lost. When the soft tissue inside a tooth becomes inflamed or infected, it aches, throbs, and keeps you up at night. Root canal therapy gently clears away the source of that pain, seals the tooth, and lets you keep it for years to come. The name has a scary reputation. The treatment itself is simply how the pain ends.

Why it doesn't hurt like the stories say

Those stories come from another era of dentistry. With modern anesthetic and an unhurried doctor, the visit is quiet and comfortable from start to finish. Dr. Burns never begins until you're completely numb, explains each step before he takes it, and checks in with you throughout. Most patients tell us afterward that it felt no different than getting a filling.

Sedation options for anxious patients

If dental fear has kept you away, you're in good company: many of Dr. Burns's most loyal patients arrived terrified. Sedation options, including IV sedation, let you rest peacefully through the entire visit while the tooth gets fixed. You tell us how relaxed you want to be, and we build your comfort plan around it. Fear should never be the reason you live with a toothache.

Why saving your natural tooth matters

Nothing chews, feels, or looks quite like your own tooth. Root canal therapy keeps it in place, which protects your bite, keeps neighboring teeth from drifting, and spares you the bigger cost of replacing a lost tooth later. When a tooth truly can't be saved, Dr. Burns will say so honestly and walk you through the alternatives. When it can, this is the treatment that saves it.
