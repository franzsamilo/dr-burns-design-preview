Frequently asked questions

The questions people ask us most

Plain answers to what's on your mind, before you ever pick up the phone.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

Most patients are surprised by how comfortable it is. You can be fully sedated with a physician anesthesiologist watching over you, so you go to sleep, wake up, and it's done. Many patients say they don't remember a thing.

How do people pay for dental implants?

Individual implants start at $1,500, and Dr. Burns shows you the full quote before anything begins, with no hidden fees. Flexible financing is available through CareCredit and Cherry, plus no-interest and low-interest health care loans, and we help you check your insurance.

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that replaces the root of a missing tooth. It integrates with the jaw bone, strengthening it and stimulating bone growth, and it supports a natural-looking replacement tooth that can last a lifetime when properly cared for.

Am I too old for dental implants?

Age alone rarely rules anyone out. What matters is your overall health and bone, and both can usually be worked with. The only way to know for sure is a 3D scan and an honest conversation with Dr. Burns.

Can I really get new teeth in one day?

Yes. Most full-arch patients leave surgery the same day with a fixed temporary smile in place. Your final smile goes in once everything has healed and settled.
