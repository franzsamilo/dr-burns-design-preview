Plenty of offices treat every smile as a one-off. The Burns Protocol runs the opposite way. It's a repeatable, high-standard process, so the care you get isn't riding on how busy the day was. Same plan, same checks, same doctor. That consistency is a big part of the 98%+ success rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

The Burns Protocol, answered straight

What is The Burns Protocol?

It's the step-by-step method Dr. Jeffrey Burns created for designing and placing a DreamSmile™. It plans beauty and bite together, uses 3D imaging to remove guesswork, and keeps one doctor on your case start to finish. The goal is a smile that looks natural, works right, and lasts.

How is it different from going to a dental chain?

At a chain you're often passed between providers and referred across town for surgery. With The Burns Protocol, planning, surgery, and lab work happen under one roof in New Market, with Dr. Burns himself. Every DreamSmile™ patient also gets his personal cell number.

Does the treatment hurt?

Most patients are surprised by how comfortable it is. You can be fully sedated with a board-certified physician anesthesiologist watching over you, so you go to sleep, wake up, and it's done. Many patients say they don't remember a thing.

How much does it cost near Harrisonburg?

À la carte pricing starts at $1,500 per item, and Dr. Burns shows you the full number before anything begins. No hidden fees, no surprise add-ons. Flexible financing is available, and your free consultation includes an honest quote for your case.

Where is Dr. Burns located, and who does he serve?

The practice is at 9626 South Congress Street in New Market, VA. Patients travel in from Harrisonburg, Winchester, Woodstock, Timberville, and across the Shenandoah Valley to be treated through The Burns Protocol.
