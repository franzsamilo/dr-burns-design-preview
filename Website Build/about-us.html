An office that doesn't feel like an office

There's a fireplace in the lounge and a mountain view from the treatment rooms. That's not decoration, it's strategy. Nervous patients relax faster in a place that feels like a home, and relaxed patients get better care. Sedation is there if you need more help than a fireplace can give.

Oral surgery without the referral shuffle

When a case calls for an oral surgeon, you don't get a phone number and a wish of good luck. Dr. James Whitney of Whitney Oral Surgery works in the same building, so your records, your doctor, and your surgeon stay under one roof from start to finish.
