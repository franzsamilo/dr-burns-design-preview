Before your first visit

Your first appointment is a conversation before it's an exam. Dr. Burns wants to hear what's bothering you and what you want, and the imaging fills in the rest. Complete your new patient forms at home and your visit starts on time, with no clipboard waiting for you in the lounge.

Insurance, without the mystery

Coverage differs plan to plan, so the safest move is a quick call to the office before your appointment. Bring your insurance card to your first visit and ask us anything about your benefits. The front desk will tell you plainly what to expect, before any work begins.

Have a coverage question? Call 540-740-8937 and just ask. Call the Office

Two ways to spread out the cost

Good dentistry shouldn't have to wait for a windfall. CareCredit offers healthcare credit you can apply for in minutes, and Cherry splits treatment into monthly payments with a short application. Ask about either one at your visit, or start from the Financing card above.
