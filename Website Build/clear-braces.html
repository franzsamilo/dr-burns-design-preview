Clear braces built for adult life

Work meetings, weddings, family photos: adult life doesn't pause for orthodontics, and with clear aligners it doesn't have to. The trays are nearly invisible from a normal speaking distance, and they come out for dinner, coffee, and the big presentation. You straighten your teeth while your calendar carries on.

Mail-order aligners vs a doctor who sees your teeth

Mail-order kits move teeth without anyone looking at your gums, your bite, or your bone. When something drifts off plan, there's a chat window. Here, the same doctor examines your mouth before treatment starts and checks the fit at every stage, catching small problems while they're still small. Your teeth are not a subscription box.
