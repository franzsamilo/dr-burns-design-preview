Understanding the system

The Burns Protocol™, explained

Exceptional smiles are carefully designed

At our dental office in New Market, Virginia, exceptional smiles don't happen by chance. They're carefully designed. That philosophy is the foundation of The Burns Protocol™, a proprietary smile-design system created by Jeffrey S. Burns, DDS to perfect the DreamSmile® process. It's the reason two patients can sit in the same chair and leave with two very different smiles, each one right for its owner.

Created by the DreamSmile® founder

Dr. Jeffrey S. Burns, DDS is the creator of DreamSmile®, and The Burns Protocol™ grew out of everything he learned building it: years of clinical experience, advanced training, and a stubborn insistence on precision dentistry. The result is a refined approach that blends science, artistry, and patient-centered care. It's not a slogan on the wall. It's how every case in this office actually runs.

What is The Burns Protocol™?

It's a comprehensive framework that guides every DreamSmile® transformation, so no detail is overlooked, from facial aesthetics to bite alignment and long-term durability. Rather than focusing only on cosmetic appearance, The Burns Protocol™ prioritizes both beauty and function. Its key principles:

  • Customized smile design tailored to the individual
  • Emphasis on facial harmony and balance
  • Careful evaluation of bite function and comfort
  • Precision planning for predictable, lasting results

How The Burns Protocol™ enhances DreamSmile®

Every DreamSmile® is built upon The Burns Protocol™. That structure lets Dr. Burns deliver consistent excellence while still customizing each smile to reflect the patient's goals and personality. In practice, you get a thoughtful consultation and planning process, clear communication and expectations at every visit, natural-looking results that feel comfortable and confident, and a smile designed to stand the test of time.

Beauty and function, planned together

A smile that photographs beautifully but can't handle a bite of steak isn't finished. Neither is a strong bite behind teeth that look pasted on. That's why facial harmony and balance are weighed alongside bite function and comfort in the same plan, never traded against each other. Bite alignment gets the same attention as the shade of a front tooth, because ten years from now, both will matter.

Choosing a process matters as much as choosing a dentist

If you're considering a smile transformation, that's the question worth sitting with. Skill varies day to day. A process doesn't. The Burns Protocol™ was designed to hold the DreamSmile® experience to a repeatable, high standard and give patients results they can trust. It's why people drive in from Harrisonburg, Winchester, Woodstock, and towns across the Shenandoah Valley to have their smiles designed in New Market.

What patients ask about The Burns Protocol™

What is The Burns Protocol™?

It's a comprehensive framework that guides every DreamSmile® transformation. The system makes sure no detail is overlooked, from facial aesthetics to bite alignment and long-term durability. Rather than focusing only on cosmetic appearance, it prioritizes both beauty and function.

Who created The Burns Protocol™?

Dr. Jeffrey S. Burns, DDS, the creator of DreamSmile®. He developed it after years of clinical experience, advanced training, and a passion for precision dentistry, with one goal: a repeatable, high-standard process that delivers beautiful, natural-looking smiles while protecting long-term oral health.

Is it only about how my smile looks?

No. Beauty and function carry equal weight. Every plan starts with customized smile design and facial harmony and balance, then adds a careful evaluation of bite function and comfort, so your smile looks natural and works the way teeth should.
