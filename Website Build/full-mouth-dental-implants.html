What full mouth dental implants actually are

Instead of one implant per missing tooth, All-on-X uses a small number of implant posts, usually four to six per jaw, to anchor a complete arch of fixed teeth. The posts work like natural tooth roots, so your new teeth stay put when you eat and your jawbone stops shrinking underneath them. One procedure replaces everything, top or bottom or both.

Dentures and implant teeth aren't even cousins

A denture rests on your gums. It slips when you laugh, blocks the roof of your mouth, and comes out at night. Worse, the bone under it keeps shrinking year after year, which is why dentures need constant relining. A fixed implant arch is screwed to posts in your jaw. It doesn't move, it doesn't come out, and you bite with close to natural strength.
