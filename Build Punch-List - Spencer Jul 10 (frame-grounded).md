# Build Punch-List: Dr. Burns Site, Spencer's Jul 10 Review

For Jed and whoever's on the build. You didn't sit in on Spencer's call, so this is everything you need to act without watching the 42 minutes. Every item is tied to a timestamp from the call and a frame from the screen recording, so you can see the exact thing Spencer saw.

## The gate

Spencer won't authorize showing Dr. Burns until every page is landing-page quality. That's the whole job. Nothing ships to the client while a page still reads like an education brochure.

## The rule he's holding every page to

Every page is a landing page: one clear value prop, an emotional connection image, a call to action, a lower-threshold call to action above the fold, then social proof. His words at 00:03:53. The selling order per page is pain, then possibility, then the path. The old live site (frame 04, 00:05:30) is "all verbiage, education only, no pain, no possibilities. It would never convert." Don't drift back toward that.

## A. Still open, do these first

**1. Kill the clinical drill and loupe images.** This is the biggest thing the frames show that's easy to miss on a page-by-page read. The Journey and Protocol sections and the doctor bio all sit on top of surgical close-ups: a drill against a masked face (frames 10, 12), a loupe-and-drill macro shot (frame 13), a skin close-up (frame 25). That breaks the locked imagery rule: full-face smiling patients and Dr. Burns in doctor attire, no drills, no surgery, nothing scary. Swap every one of these for a smiling-patient or smiling-doctor image.

**2. Replace the washed-out J.burns logo.** It's been pulled off the old site and it looks degraded (frames 03, 24, 00:09:27). The real logo file lives in the Drive already, in the Logo Design folder (frame 36). Use that file. Stop sourcing the logo from the old site.

**3. Wire the actual DreamSmile logo into the homepage "Introducing" block.** Right now that section shows the J.burns wordmark, not the DreamSmile logo Spencer shared (frames 03, 14, 00:10:02). The DreamSmile logo is in the Drive too. Place it.

**4. Scope DreamSmile back to implant pages only.** The DreamSmile watermark is bleeding onto general-dentistry pages: it's on the Dental Crowns page (frame 19) and the Wisdom Teeth page (frame 26). DreamSmile is a smile created with dental implants (00:26:28). Pull the branding off every non-implant page.

**5. Shrink or remove the trademark on the wordmark.** Spencer flagged it as clunky and big (00:09:40). On the Playground build it's a tiny superscript already, so verify it's rendering as a small entity on every page and not blowing up anywhere.

## B. Structural, needed before this counts as landing-page quality

**6. Rebuild the homepage pain-point grid.** The "Band-Aid Solution" section had oversized bullets and too much dead space, weaker than the sections Bobby designed (00:13:09). It still needs a real rebuild on the new homepage. Tighten it to a proper card grid.

**7. Put a doctor bio at the bottom of every service page.** Meet-the-doctor is one of the most visited pages when someone's deciding on surgery (00:23:42). The bio block belongs at the foot of each service page, not just the homepage.

**8. Write 15 to 30 bio variations.** Same bio verbatim on every page reads as duplicate content and takes an SEO hit (00:25:58). As you roll the bio out across pages, vary it.

**9. Kill the word "specialist" everywhere.** The Playground bio still says "MEET THE SPECIALIST" (frame 13, 00:24:47). Dr. Burns is a general dentist and the board won't allow "specialist." Use "Meet the Doctor." Grep every page for it before you call this done.

**10. Every implant page gets a low-threshold offer top and bottom.** Quiz near the top, guide at the bottom, or similar (00:27:31). The homepage quiz band is already in (frame 14), so mirror that pattern onto each implant page.
