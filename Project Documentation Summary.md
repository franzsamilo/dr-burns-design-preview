The Burns Protocol — 6-Step Patient Journey
Discovery Consultation: Comprehensive evaluation, mapping patient goals, assessing oral health, and determining implant suitability (no pressure, no obligation).
Custom Treatment Plan: A personalized roadmap designed around unique anatomy, goals, and budget, with transparent pricing and financing options.
3D Smile Preview: Advanced digital imaging allowing patients to preview exactly what their new smile will look like before any work begins.
Precision Placement: Dr. Burns personally performs every implant procedure using the latest techniques learned from the world's top specialists.
Artistry-Level Restoration: Final teeth are custom-crafted by master ceramists to match natural tooth color, shape, and translucency.
Lifetime Care Partnership: The new smile is protected by an exclusive warranty and backed by a dedicated care team for years to come.


5. Consolidated Feedback & Revision Guide (April & June 2026)
This section compiles critical revisions from Bobbie's design and copy reviews. These must be executed before final site development.
Copy & Text Revisions
Brand Nomenclature: Consistently use DreamSmile™ (one word, trademark symbol) in all references. (In cases where space is required, use Dream Smile™).
Headline Update: Change "A Dream Smile You Never Have to Hide" to "A DreamSmile™ You'll Never Have to Hide".
Homepage Bullet Points: Highlight these core advantages:
A beautiful smile designed to be healthier & longer-lasting
Proprietary procedure with a 98%+ success rate (Update any references to 95% to 98%+)
A doctor who truly cares—gives you his personal cell number
Backed by the DreamSmile™ Lifetime Warranty*
Same Day Teeth (weave in that patients get their temporary teeth on the same day)
CTAs: Add the practice phone number directly below "Schedule Your Free Consultation" and in the upper right-hand corner.
Comparison Tables (DreamSmile vs. Competitors):
Change the competitor column label from "Dental Chains" to "Traditional Dental Implants".
Replace the text "The DreamSmile" with the official DreamSmile™ logo.
Change "Hidden Fees & Upsells" to "Hidden Fees and Limited or No warranty".
Change "The price you're quoted is rarely the price you pay." to "Surprise additional costs and uncertainty if you need additional support."
Layout, Margins & Typography
Reduce Vertical Space: Gaps between sections are too large. Avoid excessive vertical scrolling. Convert long blocks of text into bullet points paired with a single patient image.
"YOUR JOURNEY" Section:
Shorten the large top margin.
Increase the size of the "YOUR JOURNEY" title for readability (essential for older demographics).
Remove "SCHEDULE YOUR FREE ASSESSMENT" CTA buttons from individual steps except for right below the final "Dream Smile Assessment" step.
Split Layouts: Do not overlay grey text or filters over background photos. Instead, use a split layout: text on one side with a solid background, and a clean, unfiltered image on the other side.
Links: Paraphrase references from external health sites (e.g., Cleveland Clinic) as native text. Hyperlink keywords inline instead of adding massive exit buttons that redirect users off-site. Remove "Read our reviews" links.
Imagery & Photo Guidelines
No Cold/Clinical Photos: Replace images showing drills, surgery, scary filling procedures, or clinical instruments. These create dental anxiety.
Positive Outcome Focus: Use warm, front-facing, high-quality photos of smiling patients (ideally showing their full face).
Dr. Burns' Photo: Change his portrait to a high-quality picture of him smiling in his professional doctor/clinical attire.
Logo Placement: Use the DreamSmile™ logo as a subtle watermark on patient smile images.
Social Proof / "As Seen On" Section:
Remove "A moment to remember and stuff about sharing flowers".
Replace it with an "As Seen On" trust-bar featuring logos of local TV and radio stations (refer to texassedationdental.com for reference layout).
Page-Specific Content Directives
Single Implant Page:
Clarify that single implants are performed under local anesthesia, but sedation is available if desired.
Use the copy "with local or sedation anesthesia".
State that "a board-certified physician anesthesiologist" will be present if sedation is selected.
Change the primary CTA button to "Call him".
Replace the word "resorb" with "shrink" (for ease of patient understanding).
Remove "For further reading" title.
Weave in before-and-after single implant photos and an explanatory diagram illustrating how the implant acts as the tooth root.
Dental Fillings Page:
Add educational text describing what a filling is, why patients choose it, and its health benefits.
Include high-quality before-and-after photos of fillings.
