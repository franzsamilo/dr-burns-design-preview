# Design Direction Brief - Multi-Agent Synthesis

Multi-agent synthesis of the Jeffrey Burns knowledge base, client/reviewer complaints, current website source, media library, wireframes, and prior design-direction analysis.

## Final read

The client is not lacking design direction. The direction is embedded in what they keep rejecting.

They want a site that feels like:

> **Bright premium confidence:** warm, readable, grey/white/teal, proof-heavy, locally trustworthy, and built to convert older dental implant patients into calls or consultations.

They do **not** want:

> Dark luxury, generic dental-template polish, clinical procedure imagery, low-contrast grey-on-grey sections, oversized scrolling layouts, or abstract “premium” design that ignores the real DreamSmile™ assets.

## Non-negotiables

| Area | Must do | Must avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Color | White, off-white, light grey, `#3e3e3e`, teal `#74bdc2` | Black-heavy sections, dark luxury, grey text on grey backgrounds |
| Typography | Larger type for older readers, readable hierarchy, Montserrat + restrained Amiri/serif | Tiny labels, ornate low-legibility type, small process text |
| Imagery | Full-face smiling patients, Dr. Burns smiling in doctor attire, before/after proof, diagrams where needed | Drills, surgery, raw mouth/procedure shots, cold clinical chairs, generic stock |
| Brand | DreamSmile™ as named product, official DreamSmile™ logo, Burns Protocol as mechanism | “Dream Smile” inconsistency, generic dental implant language |
| Layout | Compact split sections, bullets, proof every few sections, clear CTA path | Huge vertical gaps, detached text blocks, repeated CTA spam |
| Conversion | Phone in header, consultation CTA above fold, proof near CTA, final CTA stack | Treating pages as passive SEO articles |

## What the client really means by the complaints

### “Too much black”
They are rejecting black as the default premium signal. Premium here should come from clarity, trust, proof, warmth, and Dr. Burns’ authority, not from a dark luxury aesthetic.

**Directive:** use `#3e3e3e` as the darkest main brand neutral. Go lighter for variation, not darker.

### “Low contrast”
They are not asking for harsh contrast. They are asking for legibility and separation.

**Directive:** white cards on light grey sections, strong text contrast, teal accents with enough visual weight.

### “Too much scrolling”
This is a conversion complaint. The page is delaying persuasion.

**Directive:** reduce section height, tighten margins, convert paragraphs to bullets, and show proof/CTA sooner.

### “Clinical/scary images”
The site should reduce dental anxiety, not trigger it.

**Directive:** sell the outcome and safety: smiles, confidence, comfort, patient stories, diagrams. Do not lead with procedures.

### “Use actual assets”
They do not want generic mockups or placeholder patients.

**Directive:** asset-led redesign using the downloaded media library and actual DreamSmile™ assets.
